Sarı Durak — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Sarı Durak is a game published by Okan Irtis. This policy explains what information the game processes and what is done with it.

Contact: info@okanirtis.com

In short

  • Your single-player save stays on your device; it never reaches us.
  • In the phone app, ads are shown by Google AdMob. If you allow it, ads are chosen by your interests; if you don't, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play; your card details never reach us.
  • In the shared world (once it opens) your email address, in-game name and progress are stored; you can delete your account from inside the game.
  • We do not sell your information.

Single-player game

No account is needed. Your save, gems and settings are stored only on your device and never reach us. Deleting the app deletes the save. The code you get from Menu → Save backup when changing phones stays with you; we never see it.

Ads

The phone app has two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads: shown only when you tap a "watch an ad" button; you get an in-game reward in return.
  • Interstitial ads: a short ad that appears now and then on its own. Never on your first day, at most once every 15 minutes, and never with the supporter or ad-free pack.

There are no ads in the browser version or in the shared world.

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google processes some information about your device: the advertising identifier (if you allowed it), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device type and operating system, and your interaction with the ad (shown, tapped). This information does not reach us. How Google uses it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Personalised ads: on iPhone, Apple's tracking permission prompt appears when the app is first opened. If you allow it, ads are chosen by your interests; if you don't, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads). Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see Google's consent form; you can change your choice in the game under Menu → Privacy and your data → Ad privacy options.

Purchases

The game offers optional gem packs, a supporter pack and an ad-free pack. Payment is made through the App Store or Google Play; your card details never reach us. We only learn which pack was bought. Your gems are kept in the save on your device. The supporter and ad-free packs can be restored with the same store account using the "Restore purchase" button.

Feedback

The "Send feedback" button opens your device's email app with a prepared message; sending it is up to you. If you choose, the game version, device type, screen size and the last error log (if any) are added to the message. If you send it, your email address and message reach us and are used only to reply to you and fix the problem.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game sends you reminders scheduled on your own device (e.g. when your drivers finish their shift). These are set up on your device and send us nothing. In the shared world, push notifications are also sent (e.g. when your plate sells or you win a tender); for this, your device's notification address is stored on our server with your account and deleted when you sign out or delete your account. You can turn notifications off from the menu.

Shared world

The shared world has not opened yet. Once it opens and you join, the following is stored on our server:

InformationWhy
Your email addressTo send your sign-in code and recognise your account
The name and company name you enterTo show other players in rankings, co-ops, the weekly race and the plate market
Your in-game progress (cars, cash, stands)For the game to work and so you can continue on another device. Other players visiting your stand see a summary: district, number of cars, rating, net worth and special plates
Session key (in hashed form)So you don't have to request a code on every launch
Your IP addressTo limit sign-in code requests; kept for at most two days

Your email address is never shown to other players. We only send you sign-in codes, never promotional email.

Where your information is kept

The shared-world server runs on Cloudflare. Sign-in code emails are sent with Resend. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Push notifications are delivered by Apple's and Google's notification services. These companies process information to provide these services and may store it on servers outside Turkey.

We do not share your information with anyone else and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it

  • Your shared-world account and the information linked to it are kept until you delete them.
  • When a season ends, that season's progress is deleted; the top 100 of the ranking (name, company name, net worth) stays in the archive.
  • Sign-in codes are valid for 10 minutes; their records are deleted within a day.
  • Feedback emails are kept for at most one year after the issue is resolved.

Deleting your information

  • Single-player: use Menu → Reset game, or delete the app.
  • Shared world: Menu → Delete my account immediately and permanently deletes your email address, name, progress and your name in past season archives from the server. If you can't get into the game, just write to the address above.

Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you have the right to learn what information we hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. If you are in Europe, you also have your rights under the GDPR. Write to the address above to use them.

Children

The game is not designed for children. The shared world is not intended for children under 13; if we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. For important changes we will also let you know inside the game.
