Sarı Durak — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Sarı Durak is a game published by Okan Irtis. This policy explains what information the game processes, what is done with it, on what legal basis it is processed and which companies help process it. Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the data controller is Okan Irtis.

Contact: info@okanirtis.com

In short

  • Your single-player save stays on your device; it never reaches us. If you allow it, only a short summary (your in-game name and company name, district, game day, cash, number of cars) reaches us with a random device number, so we can give support; you can turn it off.
  • When it opens, the game downloads settings, announcements and events from our server. If you allow it, it sends crash reports without personal data and anonymous usage counts; you can turn both off.
  • In the phone app, ads are shown by Google AdMob. For each ad, AdMob asks several ad networks and shows the ad of the highest bidder: besides Google, AppLovin, Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize. If you allow it, ads are chosen by your interests; if you don't, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • In the phone app, Meta's, TikTok's and Google's measurement tools are used to measure whether our ads on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Google work, but only if you allow it (on iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt; on Android, the game's own question). If you allow it, that the app was installed and opened, a few game events, purchases and your advertising ID are sent to them. If you don't, nothing is sent to these three companies.
  • Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play; your card details never reach us.
  • An account is optional: your email address, your password in encrypted form, your username, your friend invite code, a cloud save of your game and your shared world progress are stored; you can delete your account from inside the game.
  • We do not sell your information.

Single-player game

No account is needed. Your save, gems and settings are stored only on your device and never reach us. Deleting the app deletes the save. The code you get from Menu → Save backup when changing phones stays with you; we never see it.

Ads

The phone app has two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads: shown only when you tap a "watch an ad" button; you get an in-game reward in return.
  • Interstitial ads: a short ad that appears now and then on its own. Not during a new player's first sessions (only after at least 3 launches and an hour of play in total; in older versions never on the first day). It appears when you come back after at least an hour away, at most once every 15 minutes; since version 1.0.10 also once each time you use "Let the manager do it", before the manager starts. Never with the supporter or ad-free pack.

The game is not played in a browser; the page that opens in a browser is only a download page and shows no ads.

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. For each ad, AdMob asks several ad networks at the same time and shows the ad of the network that bids the most. These networks are Google and the three companies below; each of them has a small piece of software (SDK) inside the phone app. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, they process this information about your device: the advertising identifier (if you allowed it), IP address and the approximate location derived from it (the game does not ask for location permission; your device's GPS location is not sent), device type, operating system, language and carrier, the app's name and version, your interaction with the ad (shown, watched, tapped) and ad loading and error information. Your name, email address, game save and in-game names are not sent to these companies. This information does not reach us person by person; we only see total impression counts and earnings.

Ad measurement (Meta, TikTok and Google)

We advertise the game on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Google. To measure how many people download and play the game from these ads, and to help the ads reach people who will enjoy the game, the phone app (iPhone and Android) contains Meta's (Facebook), TikTok's and Google's (Firebase / Google Analytics) measurement tools (SDKs). They are not in the browser.

  • They start only if you allow it. On iPhone, the permission is Apple's tracking prompt: they start if you choose "Allow". On Android, the game asks its own question at the same moment ("Allow" / "Don't allow", shown equally); they start if you choose "Allow". The question appears a little after the tutorial (or before your first ad). If you don't allow it, or before the question, these tools never start and nothing is sent to these three companies.
  • What is sent (if you allowed it): that the app was installed and opened; a few game events (finishing the tutorial, buying a second car for your fleet, reaching level 3, opening the game the day after installing it); in-app purchases (package, price, currency); device information (model, operating system version, language, time zone, screen); the app version; the IP address (and the approximate location derived from it); the advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android); for Google also a random number specific to this installation of the app (app instance ID), session information, and impression and earnings information of the ads shown by AdMob. Your name, email address, account, game save and in-game names are not sent.
  • At Google: Google signals and data sharing settings are off; event data is kept at Google for 14 months. Screen view logging is off.
  • Apple's anonymous counting (SKAdNetwork, iPhone only): if you downloaded the game from an ad, Apple tells the company that showed the ad only that an install happened and a coarse progress value (for example, whether the tutorial was finished or a purchase was made); who you are is not sent. The game writes this value to Apple itself; this sends no data to us or to any company and works regardless of the permission. Google Play's install counting (Android) is also anonymous and works regardless of the permission.
  • Europe: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, if you don't allow ad measurement in Google's consent form, these tools never start.
  • Changing and turning it off: on iPhone you can change the tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can change it with the switch under Menu → Privacy and your data; when you turn it off, measurement stops immediately. If you turn off anonymous usage statistics, measurement turns off too (completely, the next time you open the game).

Meta, TikTok and Google process this information under their own privacy policies: facebook.com/privacy/policy, tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy, policies.google.com/privacy. This information does not reach us person by person; in the ad and measurement dashboards we only see totals.

Install source (Android only)

On Android, on first launch the game reads Google Play's install information (install referrer) once to tell where you installed it from: a Google ad, a Meta ad, searching the Play Store, or another way. Only that name is kept on your phone; ad click numbers and other values are not kept and are not sent to us or any other company. No permission is needed: there is no identity.

Purchases

The game offers optional gem packs, a starter pack, a supporter pack and an ad-free pack. Payment is made through the App Store or Google Play; your card details never reach us. We only learn which pack was bought. In the single-player game your gems are kept in the save on your device. If you have an account, your purchases are recorded to your account for support and account security: for a purchase in the single-player game too, the store's signed purchase record is sent to our server and the transaction number, pack and time are kept with your account (not used for advertising). If you don't have an account, your single-player purchases don't reach us. Gems you buy while in the shared world go to your game on the server: for this the store's signed purchase record is sent to our server, and the transaction number, pack and time are kept with your account so the same purchase isn't counted twice. If you delete your account, your account is removed from this record (the transaction number stays so the same purchase can't be credited to another account). The supporter and ad-free packs can be restored with the same store account using the "Restore purchase" button.

Feedback

The message you write with the "Send feedback" button reaches our server (if the server can't be reached, your e-mail app opens instead). If you choose, the game version, device type, screen size and the last error log (if any) are added, and you can add your e-mail address for a reply. You see our reply in the game, in Menu → My support messages, and you can write again there while the conversation is open. For this, the game gives the message a random number and keeps it on your phone; our server keeps only a hash of that number, so only that phone can read the reply. We may attach a make-up gift to our reply; it goes to your game once. If you're signed in and choose so, your account is attached to the message as well (so we can find your game and help). This information is used only to reply to you and fix the problem.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game sends you reminders scheduled on your own device (e.g. when your drivers finish their shift). These are set up on your device and send us nothing. In the shared world, push notifications are also sent (e.g. when your plate sells or you win a tender); for this, your device's notification address is stored on our server with your account and deleted when you sign out or delete your account. So that rivalry notifications (e.g. when someone overtakes you in the league) don't arrive at night, your device's time zone is stored with this address. You can turn notifications off from the menu, and rivalry notifications type by type.

Home screen widget

When it goes to the background, the phone app writes a short summary for its home screen widget: your company name, the time left on the shift, the estimated earnings waiting for you and the number of things to do (in the shared world, the season and your rank). If you add the Sarı Durak widget to your home screen, it shows this summary. The summary stays on your device, in a place only this app and its widget can read; it is not sent to us and never leaves your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Legal basis

We process your information on these bases under Article 5 of the KVKK:

  • Entering into and performing a contract: your account, sign-in, cloud save and your game in the shared world; delivering the packs you buy to your game and making sure a purchase is not counted twice; push notifications in the shared world (if you allowed them); replying to your feedback and sending make-up gifts.
  • Legitimate interest (provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms): protecting the game and accounts (limits on wrong passwords and mass account creation, request limits by internet address, making sure a purchase or an invite reward is not used twice), finding and fixing errors (crash reports, connection problem reports), improving the game (anonymous usage statistics, the anonymous revenue count), giving support (the game summary for support) and keeping a free game running with ads (non-personalised ads). You can turn off crash reports, anonymous statistics and the game summary for support under Menu → Privacy and your data.
  • Explicit consent: personalised ads and ad measurement (Apple's tracking prompt on iPhone, the game's own question on Android, Google's consent form in Europe). You can withdraw your consent at any time; withdrawing does not affect the game, it only stops the processing from then on.
  • Legal obligation: if a competent authority asks for it on the basis of the law.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, cloud save and the information linked to them (including your invite code and invite link) are kept until you delete them. If you delete your account, your invite code is deleted too; the account that invited you, or that you invited, keeps only the counts and your random account number.
  • When a season ends, that season's progress is deleted; the top 100 of the ranking (name, company name, net worth) stays in the archive.
  • Feedback messages (with their conversation) are kept for at most 120 days after the conversation is closed, and at most one year in all.
  • Reports of inappropriate names in the shared world (the reported name and the reporting account) are kept for at most 180 days; if you delete your account, it is removed from the reports you made.
  • Crash reports are kept for 30 days and anonymous daily usage totals for at most 400 days.
  • Ad measurement (if you allowed it): event data in Google Analytics is kept at Google for 14 months. At Meta and TikTok, under their own policies.

Deleting your information

  • Single-player: use Menu → Reset game, or delete the app.
  • Account and shared world: Menu → My account → Delete my account immediately and permanently deletes your email address, password, usernames, cloud save, progress and your name in past season archives from the server. If you can't get into the game, just write to the address above.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to: learn whether your information is processed and, if so, ask for information about it; learn the purpose of the processing and whether it is used for that purpose; know to whom it is transferred in Turkey or abroad; ask for it to be corrected if it is incomplete or wrong, to be deleted or destroyed under the conditions in the KVKK, and for these actions to be notified to those it was transferred to; object to a result against you that arises only from analysis by automated systems; and claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

Write to the address above to use them; we reply free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board. If you are in Europe, you also have your rights under the GDPR.

Children

The game is not designed for children. Accounts and the shared world are not intended for children under 13; if we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. For important changes we will also let you know inside the game.
