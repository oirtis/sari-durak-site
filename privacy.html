Sarı Durak — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Sarı Durak is a game published by Okan Irtis. This policy explains what information the game processes and what is done with it.

Contact: info@okanirtis.com

In short

  • Your single-player save stays on your device; it never reaches us. If you allow it, only a short summary (your in-game name and company name, district, game day, cash, number of cars) reaches us with a random device number, so we can give support; you can turn it off.
  • When it opens, the game downloads settings, announcements and events from our server. If you allow it, it sends crash reports without personal data and anonymous usage counts; you can turn both off.
  • In the phone app, ads are shown by Google AdMob. If you allow it, ads are chosen by your interests; if you don't, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • On iPhone, Meta's and TikTok's measurement tools are used to measure whether our ads on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok work: that the app was installed and opened, a few game events and purchases are sent to them. The advertising identifier is sent only if you allow Apple's tracking permission. If you turn off anonymous statistics, measurement is turned off too.
  • Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play; your card details never reach us.
  • An account is optional: your email address, your password in encrypted form, your username, your friend invite code, a cloud save of your game and your shared world progress are stored; you can delete your account from inside the game.
  • We do not sell your information.

Single-player game

No account is needed. Your save, gems and settings are stored only on your device and never reach us. Deleting the app deletes the save. The code you get from Menu → Save backup when changing phones stays with you; we never see it.

Ads

The phone app has two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads: shown only when you tap a "watch an ad" button; you get an in-game reward in return.
  • Interstitial ads: a short ad that appears now and then on its own. Never on your first day, at most once every 15 minutes, and never with the supporter or ad-free pack.

The game is not played in a browser; the page that opens in a browser is only a download page and shows no ads.

Ads are shown by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google processes some information about your device: the advertising identifier (if you allowed it), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device type and operating system, and your interaction with the ad (shown, tapped). This information does not reach us. How Google uses it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Personalised ads: on iPhone, Apple's tracking permission prompt appears after the tutorial (or before your first ad). If you allow it, ads are chosen by your interests and your advertising identifier is also used for ad measurement (below); if you don't, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads). Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see Google's consent form; you can change your choice in the game under Menu → Privacy and your data → Ad privacy options.

Ad measurement (Meta and TikTok, iPhone only)

We advertise the game on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. To measure how many people download and play the game from these ads, and to help the ads reach people who will enjoy the game, the iPhone app contains Meta's (Facebook) and TikTok's measurement tools (SDKs). They are not in the Android app or in the browser.

  • When it starts: after you answer Apple's tracking permission prompt. Nothing is sent to these two companies before the prompt.
  • What is sent: that the app was installed and opened; a few game events (finishing the tutorial, buying a second car for your fleet, reaching level 3, opening the game the day after installing it); in-app purchases (package, price, currency); device information (model, operating system version, language, time zone, screen); the app version; the IP address. Your name, e-mail address, game save and in-game names are not sent.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): sent only if you choose "Allow" on the tracking prompt. If you don't, measurement still works, but the advertising identifier is not sent and both companies are told that tracking is not allowed; results are then measured only in aggregate, without identifiers.
  • Apple's anonymous counting (SKAdNetwork): if you downloaded the game from an ad, Apple tells the company that showed the ad only that an install happened and a coarse progress value (for example, whether the tutorial was finished); who you are is not sent. For this, the app tells Apple "installed" on its first launch.
  • Europe: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, if you don't allow ad measurement in Google's consent form, these tools never start.
  • Turning it off: if you turn off anonymous statistics under Menu → Privacy and your data, measurement does not start from the next launch. You can change the tracking permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Meta and TikTok process this information under their own privacy policies: facebook.com/privacy/policy, tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy. This information does not reach us person by person; in the ad dashboards we only see totals.

Purchases

The game offers optional gem packs, a starter pack, a supporter pack and an ad-free pack. Payment is made through the App Store or Google Play; your card details never reach us. We only learn which pack was bought. In the single-player game your gems are kept in the save on your device. Gems you buy while in the shared world go to your game on the server: for this the store's signed purchase record is sent to our server, and the transaction number, pack and time are kept with your account so the same purchase isn't counted twice. If you delete your account, your account is removed from this record (the transaction number stays so the same purchase can't be credited to another account). The supporter and ad-free packs can be restored with the same store account using the "Restore purchase" button.

Feedback

The message you write with the "Send feedback" button reaches our server (if the server can't be reached, your e-mail app opens instead). If you choose, the game version, device type, screen size and the last error log (if any) are added, and you can add your e-mail address for a reply. This information is used only to reply to you and fix the problem.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game sends you reminders scheduled on your own device (e.g. when your drivers finish their shift). These are set up on your device and send us nothing. In the shared world, push notifications are also sent (e.g. when your plate sells or you win a tender); for this, your device's notification address is stored on our server with your account and deleted when you sign out or delete your account. You can turn notifications off from the menu.

Home screen widget

When it goes to the background, the phone app writes a short summary for its home screen widget: your company name, the time left on the shift, the estimated earnings waiting for you and the number of things to do (in the shared world, the season and your rank). If you add the Sarı Durak widget to your home screen, it shows this summary. The summary stays on your device, in a place only this app and its widget can read; it is not sent to us and never leaves your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Where your information is kept

Our server (settings, crash reports, statistics, feedback, accounts, cloud saves and the shared world) runs on Cloudflare. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. On iPhone, ad measurement is done by Meta (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. and Meta Platforms, Inc.) and TikTok (TikTok Technology Limited and TikTok Pte. Ltd.). Push notifications are delivered by Apple's and Google's notification services. These companies process information to provide these services and may store it on servers outside Turkey.

We do not share your information with anyone else and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, cloud save and the information linked to them (including your invite code and invite link) are kept until you delete them. If you delete your account, your invite code is deleted too; the account that invited you, or that you invited, keeps only the counts and your random account number.
  • When a season ends, that season's progress is deleted; the top 100 of the ranking (name, company name, net worth) stays in the archive.
  • Feedback messages are kept for at most 120 days after the issue is resolved; those sent by e-mail for at most one year.
  • Reports of inappropriate names in the shared world (the reported name and the reporting account) are kept for at most 180 days; if you delete your account, it is removed from the reports you made.
  • Crash reports are kept for 30 days and anonymous daily usage totals for at most 400 days.

Deleting your information

  • Single-player: use Menu → Reset game, or delete the app.
  • Account and shared world: Menu → My account → Delete my account immediately and permanently deletes your email address, password, usernames, cloud save, progress and your name in past season archives from the server. If you can't get into the game, just write to the address above.

Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you have the right to learn what information we hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. If you are in Europe, you also have your rights under the GDPR. Write to the address above to use them.

Children

The game is not designed for children. Accounts and the shared world are not intended for children under 13; if we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. For important changes we will also let you know inside the game.
